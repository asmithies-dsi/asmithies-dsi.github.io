This is a Tableau Dashboard that tracks global Earthquake activity across a 30-day period.

Use the Analysis Date slider at the top right to change the time period being shown.

The dots on the map change colour and size according to the magnitude of the quake - you can zoom in/out for more details on a specific region.

You can scroll down while hovering over the bar charts to see beyond the top dozen or so locations.

Click on a heading to sort by it - toggle ascending/descending.

Go on, have a play around!
